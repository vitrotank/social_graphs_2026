OPERATOR’S HANDBOOK / READ BEFORE CONNECTING

  1. Read the sockets. IN counts incoming arrows; OUT counts outgoing arrows. Match the targets for every page.
  2. Tap a wire. Connect it, then tap again to reverse it. The wire buttons do the same job; Tab and Enter work too.
  3. Send a test signal. Clear all three rounds to finish your weekly shift. The last round has a missing target to deduce.

No timer penalties. Undo is free. Earn three stars with no hints or failed tests, two with one or two assists, and one for seeing it through. Replays keep your best score.
