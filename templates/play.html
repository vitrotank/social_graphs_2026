An incoming link means another Wikipedia page points here. An outgoing link means this page points elsewhere. Same wire, two very different jobs.

Every connection in these boards comes from the course’s frozen Marvel network. These are selected links: the targets count only the wires shown, not all links between the characters or their full-network degrees. A hyperlink is not necessarily a friendship.

Python picks the boards and checks every possible orientation to make sure there is exactly one solution. Reciprocal links are left out of the puzzles. Your completed boards are saved only in this browser when local storage is available.
