THE SCIENCE BEHIND THE GAME

THE GEOMETRY OF CULTURE · KOZLOWSKI ET AL. (2019)

Why does adding words move characters?

In count-based Bag-of-Words, every word is an isolated orthogonal axis. In dense Word2Vec embeddings, words live in a continuous 100-dimensional vector space where direction carries meaning.

When you add + magic and + sorcerer to Spider-Man, you are not merely adding tokens to a document. You are translating the character’s coordinate along the semantic direction of the mystical domain. Because words that occur in similar contexts share similar directional vectors, continuous vector addition smoothly steers your point past intermediate hybrid archetypes (like Scarlet Witch) directly toward Doctor Strange.

THE FAMOUS ANALOGY

king − man + woman ≈ queen

Mikolov et al. (2013) demonstrated that Word2Vec preserves linear regularities. Subtracting man removes gender while preserving royalty; adding woman redirects the vector to queen.

In our Marvel universe, Thor − hammer + shield ≈ Captain America works by the exact same geometric principle!
