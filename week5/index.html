WHAT COUNTS AS A MATCH?

Consecutive words in order, ignoring case, punctuation and numbers. Matches can cross sentences, but not blank-line paragraph boundaries.

Names and function words stay in. Article pairs need not represent distinct fictional people.

04 / WHAT SURPRISED US

LENGTH IS A CLUE

Longer still
needs reading.

The 166-word Life Story passage is genuine shared narration. But Spitfire and Union Jack share 103 words of bibliography, while Radian and Scaredycat share a 34-word introductory formula. A high threshold still needs context.

Removing the first paragraph reduces eight-word matches from 40,570 to 8,649, and twenty-word matches from 71 to 37. All thirteen forty-word pairs remain. Retaining numbers preserves the decline.

At twenty words, 31 longest matches occur in both first paragraphs; 44 pairs have a hyperlink. Neither establishes a passage’s meaning.

ONE MEANINGFUL LIMITATION

A match cannot tell us who copied whom.

It also misses paraphrases. Longer articles have more opportunities to overlap; formulas, quotations and references can survive strict thresholds. Our claim concerns this Wikipedia corpus, rather than the originality of Marvel stories.

05 / REPRODUCE THE EXPERIMENT

FROZEN INPUT / STANDARD-LIBRARY PYTHON

Leave a paper trail.

Definitions, exclusions and exact parameters

The 26 August 2026 archive contains all 303 Week 1 articles. Decoded filenames must match the roster; only README and directory entries are excluded. It yields 727,203 tokens and 27,020 types.

Unicode letter tokens retain internal apostrophes; curly apostrophes are normalized and case is folded. Hyphens separate words. Numbers and punctuation are omitted; names, stopwords and headings remain. No stemming or TF-IDF.

An eight-word inverted index finds every eligible pair. Runs extend until divergence; ties choose the earliest source position. Thresholds are 8–60. Removing the first blank-line-delimited paragraph recomputes all matches: a reproducible proxy for the lead.

Retaining numerals gives 40,581 pairs at eight tokens, 82 at twenty and 14 at forty. Six tests check boundaries, extension, ties, normalization, roster validation and source offsets. Labels use LLM-assisted inspection, not independent human annotation.
