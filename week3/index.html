01 / WHO HOLDS IT TOGETHER?

DEGREE, CENTRALITY & ABSENCE

The obvious answer.
The interesting runner-up.

Spider-Man really does hold the most together. Removing him shrinks the largest component from 277 to 271 pages: one deleted hero, five other pages cut off.

He also has the highest undirected degree and the highest betweenness. So, yes: the character whose absence fragments the network most is also the one on the greatest share of shortest routes.

But look at Black Widow (Natasha Romanova). She has 25 neighbours, placing her 23rd by degree. Count how often a shortest route needs her, and she rises to 8th by betweenness. Remove her, and the giant shrinks to 273: the second largest single-character loss.

What does she bridge? In this frozen link list, Blue Eagle has only her to turn to. Rockman connects to her and The Witness; The Witness connects only to Rockman. Remove Black Widow and these three pages become a singleton and a pair, detached from the main network. That is a precise structural claim. The links alone do not tell us why Wikipedia connects those characters.

This rank gap is a useful clue, not a significance test. We do not claim Black Widow is unusually central against a degree-preserving null. Our random baseline below asks a different question: how much worse is a targeted blackout than random failure?

FIELD NOTE / 03

Busy isn’t the whole story.

Degree
How many distinct neighbours does a page have?
Betweenness
What share of shortest routes between other pages passes through it? Tied routes split their credit.
Removal damage
How big is the largest connected component after a page disappears?

All three use the same undirected projection. A hyperlink in either direction counts as one connection.

02 / THE BLACKOUT EXPERIMENT

Try the blackout game ↗

Now keep pulling.

Three deletion orders. One frozen network. Target the original degree leaders, target the original betweenness leaders, or remove pages at random.

The budget changes the answer.

At 30 removals, degree leaves a giant of 222, betweenness leaves 225, and random failure leaves 247.6 on average. Betweenness does not win every round.

At 150 removals, both targeted orders leave a largest component of just 9, while random removal averages 128.0. The outcome depends on the deletion budget and whether rankings adapt.

READING THE COMPARISON

The denominator stays at 303 original pages, including isolates. Orders use the original degree or betweenness ranking; they are not recalculated after removal.

Random orders sample all 303 pages uniformly. There are 200 trials, with seed 2026.

03 / DIRECTORY ASSISTANCE

Open the route finder ↗

Four hops
from the edge.

Six degrees? Among pages that can reach Spider-Man, this exchange needs at most four.

Mahr Vehl, Thena, and Thin Man tie for the longest shortest directed route to Spider-Man: four links. Mahr Vehl’s selected route is Mahr Vehl → Genis-Vell → Captain Marvel → Machine Man → Spider-Man. These are article links, not a chain of people who have met.

Why start there? Mahr Vehl’s article describes him as the Ultimate version of Mar-Vell and mentions a game costume for Genis-Vell. That gives context for the first link: a connection between versions and adaptations can join the network just as a shared team can. It does not prove what produced every edge in the frozen snapshot.

Ignore direction, and the maximum falls to three hops, shared by 18 pages. There are 26 unreachable pages in that mode; following arrows makes 29 unreachable. We keep those failed calls visible instead of quietly leaving them out of the story.

MIND THE ARROW

A link is a one-way street.

In “Follow hyperlinks”, every step goes from the current article to an article it links to.

In “Either direction”, a step may traverse a link backwards. Each connection in the game’s result names its actual orientation.

There may be several equally short routes. We show one, breaking ties by page ID. “Longest” means the largest of these shortest distances among pages that can reach Spider-Man.

Week 3 B-side: Grunge FM ↗

Same questions.

The Week 3 B-side takes paths and centrality from Marvel’s pages to a sample of grunge musicians.

Who receives the most links, and who lies between other pages? Grunge FM compares incoming links with undirected betweenness and traces shortest routes between musicians, with or without following the arrows. These are the same Week 3 questions on a separate Wikipedia network.

The change of subject also changes the evidence. This small sample records links visible in retrieved article excerpts, so missing links can change its routes and rankings. Its results describe that sample, not the whole music scene.

01 / THE MATERIAL

One frozen roster.

The course’s frozen 26 August 2026 Marvel Wikipedia roster: 303 pages and 1,784 directed links. All characters are retained, including isolates.

The removal and centrality experiments collapse reciprocal links into one undirected edge and exclude loops. Routes offer both orientations.

02 / THE COMPUTATION

Exact paths and centrality.

Exact Brandes betweenness, with endpoints excluded and normalization over pairs from the full roster; exact breadth-first shortest paths; complete static removal trajectories; 200 seeded random permutations with pointwise empirical 5th–95th percentile bands.

No centrality is estimated by sampling. Single-removal “detached” counts surviving pages split off the original giant, excluding the character removed.

03 / THE INTERPRETATION

Links have limits.

Hyperlinks describe Wikipedia’s coverage, not friendship, strength, or influence in Marvel stories. The roster boundary and snapshot affect the answer. Live articles provide reading context and may differ from the frozen links.

This is a deletion-order comparison, not a degree-preserving centrality null or a claim that one attack strategy is optimal.
