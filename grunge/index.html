      04 / THE SMALL PRINT, AT A READABLE SIZE

      Know what’s on the tape.

      The sample

      A selected sample of musicians listed directly in Wikipedia’s Grunge musicians category. We record visible hyperlinks in browser-retrieved article excerpts. This is not a complete category crawl.

      Retrieved in this project’s frozen snapshot. Subcategories, bands and list pages are outside the sample. Links to people outside the roster are also excluded.

      The caveat is part of the result

      A Wikipedia hyperlink is editorial context, not proof of friendship, collaboration or influence. The excerpt sample misses links elsewhere in each article; missing edges can alter paths, components and centrality. The rankings describe this artifact only.

      We remove self-links and duplicate edges. The route finder uses breadth-first search. Betweenness is exact for the undirected sample, with endpoints excluded and normalization by (n − 1)(n − 2) / 2.
